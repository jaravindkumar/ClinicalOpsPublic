# Dark Enterprise Layout

v0.7 applies a dark enterprise SaaS visual system across ClinicalOps Intelligence Studio.

## Design choices
- Deep navy/black background
- Cyan and purple clinical intelligence accents
- Dark sidebar
- Glass-style panels
- Dark dashboard cards
- Status pills for risk, warnings and resolved items
- Developer views kept in expanders

## Portfolio rationale
The dark layout makes the product look more like an enterprise AI workflow platform rather than a simple Streamlit prototype.
