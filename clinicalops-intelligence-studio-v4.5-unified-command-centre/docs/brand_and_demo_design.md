# Brand and Demo Design

v0.6 turns ClinicalOps Intelligence Studio from a working prototype into a portfolio-ready enterprise SaaS demo.

## Design goals

- Clear enterprise healthcare AI positioning
- Dark clinical-operations sidebar
- White dashboard cards
- Blue clinical trust palette
- Executive summaries before developer views
- Guided demo flow for interviews

## Demo Mode scenarios

1. Abnormal imaging follow-up
2. Medication safety monitoring
3. Postnatal infection pattern
4. Mental health escalation

## Portfolio message

The product demonstrates that medical AI should be embedded into reviewable workflows, not used as an autonomous clinical decision-maker.
